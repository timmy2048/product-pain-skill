# TikTok playbook

Use TikTok for short-form demand language: what people ask for under videos, which hooks already run, and whether a pain is already being sold.

## Where to search

- Keyword search: `https://www.tiktok.com/search?q={query}`
- URLs from `scripts/build_search_urls.py`

Look at public video captions, on-screen text, and public comments. For reverse demand, prioritize comments like "where can I buy this," "I need this for…," "why doesn't it…," or "I would buy if…"

## What counts

- Repeated comment themes, not a single joke
- Creators already demonstrating a workaround that a product could replace
- Hooks that keep appearing for the same pain (useful later for positioning, not as proof of profit)

## Ads and keywords

TikTok keyword search is a free discovery tool. Paid ad-library coverage for TikTok is not required in v1. If you also open Meta Ad Library for the same keywords, keep those findings in the ads section of the report, and do not mix platforms.

## Access

Use public search and public comments only. Do not use unofficial downloaders or comment scrapers. If a video or region is unavailable, record the gap.
