# Google Ads Transparency Center playbook

Google Ads Transparency Center is a required **gate 5** check, complementary to Meta Ad Library. It is a free public channel for seeing whether competitors advertise on Google (search, display, YouTube, and other Google surfaces that the Center shows), what the visible ad copy says, and whether a visible landing destination looks like a brand site or Amazon.

It does **not** prove pain or paid demand. It cannot replace Reddit or YouTube for pain, or Amazon reviews and shelf proxies for demand. Do not treat it as a social evidence channel.

## Official start URL only

- Home: `https://adstransparency.google.com/`
- Search: `https://adstransparency.google.com/search`

Open these official pages in a browser. Do not call undocumented internal search endpoints, and do not invent query-string APIs. Official help states that you search by **advertiser name or website** and may filter by serving region and date: [About advertiser verification / Ads transparency](https://support.google.com/My-Ad-Center-Help/answer/12155361).

Do not sign in to a paid Google Ads account and do not spend money. A personal Google sign-in is not required to try the public pages; if a sign-in wall or script challenge appears, record the gap.

## How to search

1. Open the official start or search page.
2. Search each of the top 3–5 Amazon sellers by **advertiser or brand name**, then again by **website or domain** when you have one (including an Amazon storefront or brand site from the gate 5 checklist). Advertiser legal names can differ from the consumer brand; try both.
3. When the UI offers filters, set the serving region to the target market (US, UK, or AU) and note the date range used.
4. To check a category, look up the known competitor advertisers and domains in that category. The official tool does not document a keyword-bid search. If category language appears in visible ad copy, record it as a copy theme, not as proof of the keywords they buy.
5. If the first query returns nothing, try an alternate name or domain. "No results found" is not the same as "they never advertise," and it is not the same as a timeout.

## What to record

For each advertiser you looked up, write down:

- Ads present or not, plus the region and date filter you used
- Copy themes (claims, pain hooks, offers) that you can actually see
- Landing type when it is visible: brand site, Amazon, or unknown
- Format or last-shown date only if the page shows them

Do not invent ad counts, spend, targeting, or keywords. Absence of results for one query is not proof that no ads exist.

## Coverage and failures

List Google Ads Transparency Center in the report's **Channel coverage** section (returned or failed), alongside Meta Ad Library. If the page times out, is blocked, or returns an empty shell, mark this part of gate 5 **unverified**. Do not invent ads or copy to fill the gap.
